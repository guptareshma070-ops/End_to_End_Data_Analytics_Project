# HR Employee Distribution Report (SQL + Power BI End-to-End)

## 📌 Project Overview
This is a comprehensive, multi-page end-to-end data analytics project focused on Human Resources. The project handles a large-scale dataset of **approx. 22,000+ daily employee records**. I utilized SQL to clean and transform the raw database, and built a 2-page interactive dashboard in Power BI to track workforce demographics and department turnover rates.

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Business Questions
The HR department managed a massive database of 22,000+ records but lacked a visual system to track employee metrics, gender gaps, and turnover patterns. To solve this, this project answers the following core business questions:
* What is the exact headcount and gender distribution across the company?
* How many employees work from Headquarters versus Remote locations?
* Which states have the highest concentration of employees?
* What is the racial and ethnic breakdown of the workforce?
* Which age groups and genders dominate different departments?
* What is the average length of employment (tenure) for staff?
* Which departments suffer from the highest employee termination rates?
* How has the employee growth rate changed over the years (2000-2020)?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Used for initial raw data verification and checking column structures.
* **SQL (Database Management):** Wrote data cleaning scripts to handle duplicates, fix missing values, format dates, and aggregate data.
* **Power BI Desktop:** Imported the structured SQL data, built a relational data model, and designed a 2-page interactive dashboard.

## ⚙️ Key Features Executed
* **SQL Data Cleaning:** Handled high-volume database text formatting and column normalization.
* **SQL Querying:** Structured complex queries to pull employee counts, state distributions, and attrition rates.
* **Multi-Page Dashboard Design:** Separated findings into Page 1 (Workforce Demographics) and Page 2 (Department Turnover Analysis) using sync slicers.

## 📊 Comprehensive Insights (From Page 1 & Page 2)

### Page 1: Workforce Demographics & Distribution
* **Total Headcount:** The company manages a large workforce of 17K active employees.
* **Gender Balance:** The gender split is relatively even, with Male employees leading slightly at 8.9K compared to 8.1K Female employees.
* **Work Mode:** A massive 74.97% (13K employees) are based at the Headquarters, while 4K employees operate Remotely.
* **Geographical Layout:** Core states like Michigan, Ohio, and Pennsylvania hold the highest density of employees on the map visual.
* **Diversity Metrics:** White employees represent the largest group (5.0K), followed by Two or More Races (2.9K) and Black/African American (2.8K).
* **Historical Growth:** Employee hiring saw a steady, sharp rise over a 20-year period (2000–2020), peaking near 2020.

### Page 2: Age Distribution & Turnover Analysis
* **Dominant Age Group:** The workforce is mature, with the 35–44 age group being the largest (5.1K), followed by 25–34 (4.9K) and 45–54 (4.8K).
* **Department Attrition:** The Auditing department has the highest termination/turnover rate at 20.00%, followed closely by Legal at 17.06%.
* **Lowest Attrition:** The Marketing department shows the highest stability with a lower termination rate of 11.04%.
* **Tenure Metric:** The average length of employment for an employee in the organization is stable at 8 years.
